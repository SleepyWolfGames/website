About me

I am SleepyToast. I am an autodidact, writer, programmer, among other things. And, if you can't tell from the design of this website, I am also a bit of a minimalist.

Most of my skills are self taught through the internet or occational textbook. This is what the word autodidact means - that I'm self-taught. Most of my time is spent either acquiring new skills or honing old ones. I like learning almost anything (like how the dot over a lowercase "i" or "j" is called a "tittle"), but I mainly focus on stuff that I already have skills in, like the aforementioned writing and programming.
